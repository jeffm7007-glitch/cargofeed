Common questions

Why is there a range instead of one price?

Container prices move with steel costs, freight cycles, and local depot inventory. Two identical boxes an hour apart can price differently. The range is the honest answer; the phone quote is the exact one.

What's the difference between cargo-worthy and as-is?

Cargo-worthy guarantees wind and water tight with a solid, safe floor. As-is makes no guarantee — you inspect (or gamble) and may need floor patches or door work. The gap is usually hundreds of dollars.

Does delivery include placement?

Tilt-bed delivery usually includes sliding the container off the bed to ground level where the truck can reach. Tell the driver about gates, slopes, and soft ground — none of those are calculator problems, they're phone problems.
